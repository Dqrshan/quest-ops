# Product: QuestOps

QuestOps turns a live event into a game. Organizers set up stations (quests) marked with QR codes, players walk the venue scanning them on their phones, and a venue screen shows the standings live. Built for Kiroween 2026, Frankenstein category: event software, game mechanics, QR codes and generative AI stitched into one creature.

## People and where they are

- **Organizer:** at a laptop before the event, on a phone or laptop during it, under pressure. Needs to steer the night in seconds.
- **Player:** on a phone, standing, one hand, bad signal, in a dark room. No account, no install.
- **Venue screen:** read from several metres away in a dim hall.

## Terms (use these, consistently)

- Event, station (a quest at a place), team, player, venue screen, console, round, announcement.
- Station types: **scan** (reach it), **answer** (enter a codeword or answer), **team** (enough teammates must scan; the team earns a bonus).

## Rules that always hold

- Never invent customers, testimonials, logos or usage numbers. The demo event is labelled as simulated wherever its figures appear.
- Quiz answers never leave the server.
- Controls name their action in plain words, even when the surrounding copy is in character.
- Every error says what happened and what to do next.
